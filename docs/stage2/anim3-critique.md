# Animations, round three: what I found wrong in my own films, and what I did

Each animation was rendered whole, looked at as frame sheets at 4 and 1 frames a second and as single frames, and held against both studies' rules and the pinned notes from round two. This is the list, in the order I found things. "Fixed" means fixed and looked at again.

## The pieces, before the films

1. **Vapour as pale puffs with an orange core read as "dots" again.** At the section's scale a core 4 px across is an orange dot, the very thing pinned. Fixed: the heat a puff holds is hidden (as the script says), shown only as a faint warm halo; heat appears as triangles only when it flows, leaving each puff as it turns into drops.
2. **The first cloud was a white blob**, all lit face, no billows. Fixed: each billow drawn back to front with its own shaded rim, body and lit face, over a deep shade, so the front ones overlap the ones behind with a dark scallop.
3. **The anvil read as a string of beads.** Fixed: bigger, flatter billows in three overlapping rows.
4. **The cloud grew as rows of puffs appearing in place.** Fixed: one bubble first over the rising air, then billows rising into place from below (each comes up 70 units as it grows), the middle first at each height and then out to the sides.
5. **The column of rising air had a hard-edged band behind it.** Fixed: a soft band, fading at its edges, foot and top.
6. **Water molecules sat in one row** (a bug in how their rows were spread). Fixed.
7. **Fast air molecules near warm ground drew long sticks** (their trail was taken from the wrong clock). Fixed: the trail follows the same blended clock as the molecule, and is short.
8. **The split's two labels collided over the wall.** Fixed: each points outward from its own side.
9. **Wind streaks on the globe were tiny ticks; their joins showed as beads.** Fixed: longer comets that thin and fade behind a bright head, drawn without round joins.
10. **The circulation loops were too small to read** (a 110 by 145 px box on a phone). Fixed: for the Hadley cell the globe is zoomed right in and pushed far off to the left, so only its edge shows and the loop fills most of the picture; it pulls back out for the other two loops "like gears".
11. **The molecules close-up looked flat and the same for a minute.** Fixed: bigger glossy molecules, a far layer of small dim ones drifting slower behind (depth), and the scenes vary between pushes, a wall, a flow, a bubble and heat.

## How wind works (first full look)

12. **The opening frame showed the player's controls.** Fixed in the film tool (the controls aren't drawn in films; on the phone they fade as before).
13. **The closing storm never appeared**: it was hung on the second-last word, so it arrived as the film ended. Fixed: it sets off with "these winds" and rides for the rest of the line, with a longer hold.
14. **The 30° line lingered on the globe long after it mattered.** Fixed: cleared when the winds are shown as if the Earth stood still.
15. **The two "swerve" demonstrations appeared on the line's last word** and were gone a moment later. Fixed: they draw with "Either way" and "north", each named ("Heading south", "Heading north"), and "To its right" lands on its word.
16. **The High sign over the Sahara stood at the picture's very edge** once the globe was moved aside. Fixed: it stands in the eastern Sahara, inside the picture.
17. **Route names were the old white plates**, while every other term is a violet pill. Fixed: in a scene a route's name is the same pill.

## The storm's engine (first full look)

18. **Zooming back out of the lens left a dark disc** (the close-up shrinking away read as a hole). Fixed: the lens's white rim rides the circle as it shrinks, and as it grows going in.
19. **Heat triangles sat on the cloud's face like pepperoni.** Fixed: they stream outward from its edge and are drawn behind it, so they're seen leaving it; the cloud's own colour warms instead.
20. **The tall cloud was cut off at the top** in the wide framing. Fixed: the wide framing has more room above.
21. **The water's cooling, as molecules leave, was too slight to see.** Fixed: the water's warm tint is stronger, so the colour shift (with its overshoot) reads.

## The spin, and the way they go (first full look)

22. **The swerve demonstration's two pairs of arrows and their names piled up together, over the ground-speed arrows.** Fixed: the pairs are farther apart, the ground-speed arrows go first, and "To its right" is named between them.
23. **A scene's globe floated on flat navy.** Added: a quiet field of stars far behind it, turning with the world, for depth.

## Looking at the voiced films, full size

24. **Heat triangles had an inner outline and read as warning signs.** Fixed: one solid rounded triangle, lighter toward its tip, with its soft glow.
25. **In the close-ups the floor, where the pushes land, sat under the caption.** Fixed: the ground and the water's surface sit well above where the caption goes.
26. **Vapour puffs read as grey smoke** over the violet sky (pale cyan at half strength turns grey there). Fixed: brighter, a soft cyan rim under a pale body, so they read as vapour.
27. **Heat triangles fading out turned brown** at low strength. Fixed: they shrink as they fade.
28. **"Falling behind" was too small to see**: the dotted aim and the bent path sat in a corner of a whole globe. Fixed: the camera closes in on them.
29. **The two swerves and their names crowded each other** in the wind animation (and "To its right" sat on "Heading north" in the spin). Fixed: the pairs are 40 degrees apart, the camera closes in, and "To its right" sits between and below them.
