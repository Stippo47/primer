# Animations, round four: what I found wrong in my own work, and what I did

The brief is their notes on round three (DECISIONS, 2026-11-20, "animations round three on the phone"). Each changed piece was drawn on its own first (a page of clouds at every level, the loops on the globe, the parcel, the close-up), then each changed section was rendered as a film with its sound and looked at as frame sheets at 1 and 1.5 frames a second and as full-size single frames. "Fixed" means fixed and looked at again.

## The cloud

1. **My first cloud was a fish-scale pile**: thirteen rows of five small billows, each with its own lit cap, read as scales, and the outline as an egg. Fixed: far fewer, bigger billows (rows of four and three), and the three tones drawn as three big shapes (the whole silhouette in shade; every billow nudged toward the light for the body, so shade stays only along the edges away from it; the upper billows shrunk and nudged further for the lit face), so the lit top meets the body in one scalloped line.
2. **Rows of three lined up into columns of white balls** up a tall tower. Fixed: rows staggered; no lit face on the billows down the shaded side, so that side is one lavender body.
3. **The anvil as a flat ellipse read as a flying saucer**, and its lit face ended in a straight edge across the tower. Fixed: a plate that flares out of the tower's top, farther one way (downwind), a deep shaded underside, low swells on top, and a lit face with a curved lower edge.
4. **A heat-filled cloud turned all peach.** Fixed: the warm tint at about two thirds of the strength; the heat shows as a glow behind it and warm triangles rising through it.
5. **The lower billows and the base**: a flat shaded band along the base and a deeper body tone low down read as light from above; kept.

## The loops of air

6. **Each loop's tinted fill went muddy brown over the navy.** Fixed: a faint light fill, every loop in the moving-air colour.
7. **The equator's cloud was drawn under the loop's fill**, and the loop's rising side ran beside it rather than through it. Fixed: fills first, then the cloud, then the dashes; the Hadley loop starts at the equator, so its rising side goes up through the cloud.
8. **Names on leader lines over the globe collided with the High sign.** Fixed: each name built out to the right of its loop.
9. **The equator's storm cloud, lying on its side out from the edge, grew an anvil that read as arms** on a ghostly block. Fixed: there it stops short of an anvil (a towering cumulus), a little smaller, the loop's rising side running up through it.

## The trade winds

10. **The speed arrows ran off the right edge**, so their tips (the whole point) couldn't be compared, and the two number chips piled up at the edge. Fixed: shorter arrows, and the camera set so the parcel starts left of the middle and both tips stay on screen as it drifts west; the chips sit apart, above and below.
11. **The aim's name sat halfway down the dotted line**, right where the parcel passes. Fixed: it's a built label at the aim's end, cleared before "fallen behind" is named at the parcel's end.
12. **"Friction" reshaped the path already taken** (the whole path was worked out from the current friction, so the parcel slid east). Fixed: the path is history; friction changes only its speed from then on.

## The engine

13. **The rising bubble was tiny in a wide, empty sky**, no sea in sight. Fixed: a closer framing (`base`) that holds the sea, the bubble's whole climb and the cloud's first heap, so the bubble, the lens into it and the heap it becomes are all in one shot.
14. **The cloud towered on the line's last word**, so it grew during the next line. Fixed: it starts to tower with "rises".
15. **Heat triangles flying off the drops faded to brown outlines.** Fixed: they shrink as they fade.
16. **The condensing close-up was crowded with air molecules.** Fixed: fewer, so the vapour, the drops and the heat leaving them read.
17. **The towering cloud's top ran off the top of the picture** in the wide framing (its billows and the anvil's swells stand above the tower's height). Fixed: the wide framing holds more sky.
18. **With heat let go, the whole lit face of the cloud turned peach**, the tower like candyfloss. Fixed: a lighter warm tint; the heat reads from the glow behind it and the triangles rising through it.

## The trade winds, again

19. **The parcel's speed arrows and their numbers sat under the aim's name and, later, under "fallen behind"**. Fixed: the aim's name goes as the parcel sets off; while the fall behind is named, the parcel shows only its path, and its speeds come back for friction.
20. **The fall behind put the parcel at the picture's very edge.** Fixed: the camera a little farther west.
21. **"Seen from the ground, it blows from the east" showed an empty globe for two seconds**: the winds came on the line's last word. Fixed: they come with "seen" and swing to blow from the east with "blowing".

## The end of the spin

22. **Looked at frame by frame**: the storm over cooler water shrinks, loosens, greys and fades over about four seconds before the camera leaves; the storm reaching land comes in along one even track and fades over land over about three; nothing jumps. Each storm turns counterclockwise (cropped frames half a second apart).

## The captions and the voice

23. **The captions' pages**, read across every changed section: each page is a phrase (a sentence's end always ends a page; a comma once the page is half full), never more than two lines on the iPhone 11, and the halo keeps them readable over the molecules, the sea and the bright cloud. The CC button shows beside the speaker whenever the controls are up.
24. **The voice start** can't be seen on a film: the tests stand in for the phone (a clip loading for four seconds, a phone that wants a tap and says nothing, one quiet clip among voiced ones), and each passes.

## Still open (only the phone can say)

- Whether the new cloud reads as a cloud on the phone's own screen, and whether the warm tint of a cloud letting its heat go is too much or too little.
- Whether the parcel's two speed arrows and their numbers make "it keeps its speed, the ground speeds up" land, or whether a ride-along view is still wanted.
- Whether captions without a plate stay readable in bright sun, and whether two lines at a time reads calmly or changes too often.
- Whether "No sound played" was the slow first clip found here: the fix waits for a slow clip and asks for a tap where one is wanted, but only the phone can show the cause was this one.
- Whether the calm spin (about seven seconds a turn) reads as spinning without distracting from the words.
